## 错误组织 ❌

```
ClientRust/src/
└── scenes/
    └── game_scene/
        └── tile_texture_manager.rs  ❌ 错误位置!
```

- 违反 C# 模块对应: `Client.MirGraphics.Libraries.MapLibs` → 却放在 `scenes/game_scene`

- 命名不对应: `TileTextureManager` vs `MapLibs`
